This page lists public repositories and documentation relevant to my current interests. Descriptions summarize the public materials linked below and do not claim completed research results beyond those materials.

## Current Research

### [LeanArabic](https://github.com/hw4375-collab/Iris)

A Lean 4 feasibility project for representing a published fragment of Arabic pregroup grammar and producing machine-checkable grammatical derivations. The public repository documents both its proof-producing reducer baseline and its current scope limits.

## Research Software

### [FalconVerifier](https://github.com/hw4375-collab/FalconVerifer)

A public software project for checking formalized reasoning steps with Lean 4 and recording an assurance trace. Its documentation explicitly distinguishes verified, refuted, unknown, and ill-formed outcomes, and includes a semantic-faithfulness audit for certain LLM translations.

### [ProofLens](https://github.com/hw4375-collab/prooflens-kmp)

A Kotlin Multiplatform application for proposing and checking Lean 4 proofs across Android, desktop, and web targets. The repository documents a client/server design in which the Lean kernel is the proof-checking authority.
