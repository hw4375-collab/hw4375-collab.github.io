_Mathematical Foundations of Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence_

## About Me

I am a master's student at **New York University Abu Dhabi**, with an academic background in pure mathematics.

My research interests lie at the intersection of type theory, higher category theory, formalized mathematics, and artificial intelligence.

I am particularly interested in how mathematical structures can provide foundations for reliable machine reasoning, and how the meaning of mathematical statements can be preserved when translated between natural language and formal systems.

While proof assistants can mechanically verify the correctness of formal derivations, a fundamental question remains: does a formally verified statement faithfully represent the mathematical statement originally intended?

This distinction between formal correctness and semantic faithfulness motivates much of my current work.

## Research Interests

### Mathematical Foundations

Dependent Type Theory, Homotopy Type Theory, Higher Category Theory, and Formalized Mathematics.

### AI for Formal Mathematics

Autoformalization, Mathematical Reasoning, Semantic Faithfulness, and Machine-Checked Proofs.

### Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence

Formal Verification, Reliable Reasoning Systems, and Verification of AI Agents.

## Current Research

My current research explores the relationship between mathematical meaning, formal representation, and machine-verifiable reasoning.

I am especially interested in:

- Developing and studying formal representations of mathematical reasoning.
- Investigating semantic faithfulness in AI-assisted mathematical formalization.
- Exploring type-theoretic and homotopy-theoretic approaches to formalized mathematics.
- Applying formal verification techniques to the reliability of AI reasoning systems.
