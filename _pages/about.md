I am a master's student in the **MSc in Interdisciplinary Data Science and Artificial Intelligence** program at **New York University Abu Dhabi**. With an academic background in mathematics, I work at the intersection of mathematical foundations, formal methods, and artificial intelligence.

My research is guided by a simple question: how can mathematical structure make machine reasoning more reliable? I am particularly interested in dependent and homotopy type theory, higher category theory, formalized mathematics, and AI systems that can reason with verifiable mathematical objects.

## Research interests

- Dependent Type Theory and Homotopy Type Theory
- Higher Category Theory and Formalized Mathematics
- AI Reasoning, Autoformalization, and Semantic Faithfulness
- Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence

## Current directions

- **Formal mathematical language.** Studying representations that connect ordinary mathematical practice with proof assistants.
- **Structural reasoning.** Exploring the role of type-theoretic and categorical ideas in reliable reasoning systems.
- **Semantic faithfulness.** Investigating how formal methods can help keep machine-checked representations aligned with their intended meaning.
- **Verification-aware AI.** Exploring how formal methods can support more reliable AI reasoning and AI-agent verification.
